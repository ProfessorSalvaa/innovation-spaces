# LSHC Experience Arena: voice-over script

One clip per line. Suggested file names in brackets. Warm, calm, curious; about 140 words per minute.

## [lshc-welcome.mp3]
Welcome to the LSHC Experience Arena. This is a living model of the human body, and of how Life Sciences & Healthcare come together around it. Drag to turn it, and touch anything that glows.

## [lshc-idle.mp3]
Psst. Come closer. Turn the body, touch what glows, and see how Life Sciences & Healthcare connect.

## AI-led discovery  [lshc-discover.mp3]
Every new medicine starts as an idea. AI reads research papers, lab results and molecular data at a scale no team could, then points scientists to the targets most worth testing.

## AI-assisted diagnostics  [lshc-diagnose.mp3]
Scans and tests produce huge amounts of data. AI helps radiologists and pathologists spot subtle patterns sooner, and flags the urgent cases first, so the right people can act faster.

## Patient-centred care  [lshc-care.mp3]
Great care feels personal. When records, appointments and support are joined up, patients repeat themselves less, and clinicians spend more time with the person in front of them.

## Connected health  [lshc-connect.mp3]
A watch, a patch, a sensor at home. Connected devices stream readings to the care team, so small changes get noticed early, and a call can happen before a crisis does.

## Precision medicine  [lshc-precision.mp3]
No two people share the same genetic code. Bringing genomic and clinical data together helps match each person to the therapy most likely to work for them.

## Smarter clinical trials  [lshc-trials.mp3]
Clinical trials prove whether a new treatment works. Digital tools help find the right participants, let more people join from home, and spot safety signals early, so good treatments arrive sooner.

## Advanced therapies  [lshc-therapies.mp3]
Cell and gene therapies use a patient’s own cells, or corrected genes, to treat disease at its source. They are complex to make, so data, quality and logistics matter as much as the science.

## Plant to patient  [lshc-delivery.mp3]
A medicine is only as good as its journey. Smart manufacturing and a traceable supply chain keep every dose safe, on time, and on the shelf when a patient needs it.

## Finale  [lshc-done.mp3]
You have explored the whole picture, from the first idea in the lab to the patient at home. That is Life Sciences & Healthcare, joined up.
